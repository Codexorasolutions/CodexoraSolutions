1Introduction

Codexora Solutions ("Codexora," "we," "us," or "our") designs and builds websites for schools, colleges, shops, restaurants, clinics, and other local businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through www.codexorasolutions.online (the "Site") and while delivering our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using our Site or engaging us for a project, you agree to the practices described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the Site or share information with us.

2Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Name, phone number, email address, and business name, when you fill out our contact form or enquiry form
  • Details about your business and project requirements (pages needed, style preferences, content, images, logos) shared with us for building your website
  • Messages you send us over WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • Payment and billing details when you purchase a plan (we do not store full card numbers — these are processed by our payment partners)

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, used only to keep the Site working properly and to understand general usage
  • Form submissions are verified using Google reCAPTCHA to prevent spam

3How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes for your website project
  • To design, build, and deliver the website or service you've asked for
  • To communicate project updates, previews, and support via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • To process payments and maintain billing records
  • To improve our Site, services, and pricing based on general trends
  • To prevent spam, fraud, or misuse of our contact forms

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4WhatsApp & Communication

We use WhatsApp Business as our primary channel for client communication, project updates, and support. When you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one. Please avoid sending sensitive information (like full payment card numbers) over chat.

6Cookies

Our Site may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember simple preferences and to allow embedded services (like reCAPTCHA or maps) to function correctly. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not work as intended.

7Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us, including access controls on shared documents and secure form validation. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8Data Retention

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as needed to complete your project, provide ongoing support/maintenance under your plan, and to meet reasonable business, accounting, and legal requirements. You may request deletion of your data as described below.

9Your Rights & Choices

  • You can ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • You can ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • You can ask us to delete your information, subject to any ongoing project or legal obligations
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time by telling us on WhatsApp or email

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

10Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for business owners and administrators (e.g. school/college staff) and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Student or faculty information provided by a school/college client for their website is handled solely on that client's instructions, for the purpose of building the requested pages (e.g. staff directories, results).

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
